# QA Tools overview

Beefree SDK's QA tools help your end users catch content problems and confirm their designs meet quality, accessibility, and brand standards before they ship. This page is your starting point for every QA feature in the SDK.

Use the sections below to find the right tool for what you need, or jump straight to a specific feature.

Beefree SDK offers two QA tools. Each adds a shield icon to the builder that opens a dedicated results panel when clicked. Quality Check flags whether designs **function** like they should, while Brand Check flags whether they **look** like they should.

## Quality Check: built-in content quality and accessibility

[Quality Check](quality-check.md) evaluates email and page designs against quality and accessibility best practices. It flags things like missing alt text, insufficient color contrast, missing links, and oversized images or HTML.

Quality Check is available on all paid plans (Essentials and above) at no extra cost. Advanced customization, including custom thresholds and check selection, requires a Superpowers or Enterprise plan.

[Learn more about Quality Check](quality-check.md)

## Brand Check: your own brand rules

Quality Check applies a single, fixed set of rules to every design. [Brand Check](brand-check.md) lets your users define their own design rules as presets: single values or combinations (like background and text color together) covering color, typography, spacing, buttons, links, and headings. Every email is checked against those presets after creation. Anything off-brand is flagged along with a suggested fix, and your users decide whether to apply it with a single click.&#x20;

Brand Check is available for email designs on Superpowers and Enterprise plans.

[Learn more about Brand Check](quality-check.md)

## Build your own

Brand Check and Quality Check are ready to use: activate them with a single click and get a built-in UI, with no implementation effort and no additional usage cost. Both tools are backed by the [Check CSAPI endpoint](../apis/content-services-api/check.md). If you'd rather build a fully custom QA experience, you can build it from scratch on top of the Check CSAPI endpoint and develop your own UI, additional CSAPI usage costs will apply.
